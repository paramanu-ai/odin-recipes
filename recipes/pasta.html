Creamy Garlic Parmesan Pasta

Home

Description

A quick and comforting pasta dish tossed in a creamy garlic and Parmesan sauce. Perfect for an easy weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

For the Pasta

  • Fettuccine or spaghetti — 350 g
  • Salt — 1 tsp
  • Water — 2.5 liters

For the Sauce

  • Butter — 3 tbsp
  • Garlic, minced — 4 cloves
  • Heavy cream — 250 ml
  • Parmesan cheese, grated — 100 g
  • Black pepper — ½ tsp
  • Salt — ½ tsp
  • Fresh parsley, chopped — 2 tbsp
  • Pasta water — ½ cup

Steps

  1. Cook the pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions until al dente. Reserve ½ cup of pasta water before draining.
  2. Prepare the garlic: Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Make the sauce: Pour in the heavy cream and bring it to a gentle simmer. Cook for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add Parmesan: Gradually stir in the grated Parmesan until melted and the sauce becomes smooth.
  5. Combine: Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss until evenly coated. Add a little reserved pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
  6. Season: Add salt and black pepper to taste.
  7. Serve: Garnish with fresh parsley and additional Parmesan. Serve immediately.
